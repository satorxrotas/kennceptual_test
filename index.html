— Process

Built the way hardware is built. Not the way fashion usually is.

Every piece starts as raw hide, not a print file or a bolt of fabric off a shelf. We cut around the leather's own flaws instead of hiding them — which is why no two pieces leave the shop looking exactly the same.

The hardware and stitch work are what people touch the most, so they're what we obsess over: solid, not stamped, finished to sit flush with zero play.
